# Introduction to Calculus of Variations

**Contents**

* [**Basics**](calculus-variations:basics). Definition of a functional, definition of a variation, stationariety principle: Lagrange equations, Euler-Beltrami equation

* [**Examples**](calculus-variations:examples). Shortest path between two points; Fermat principle; Lagrangian formulation of classical mechanics, and special relativity

* [**Extra**](calculus-variations:extra). 
   * Variational principle must have an invariant form, i.e. it must not depent on arbitrary choices of coordinates or parameters that have no geometrical or physical invariant nature

   * Parametrization of the integral with a parameters whose value depends on the integral itself. E.g.: arc-length parametrization of the length of a curve; arc-length parametrization of time in Fermat principle;...
